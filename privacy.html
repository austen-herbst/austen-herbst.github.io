Privacy policy

Austen Home Bridge is a personal connection between ChatGPT and Google's official Home MCP server. It is intended for use by its owner only.

Data and use

When the owner authorizes the connection, the Google Home permission allows the connected ChatGPT account to inspect Home structures, devices, states, and history, and to request supported device controls. Responses may include details about the owner's home and may appear in ChatGPT conversations. This data is used to answer the owner's requests and carry out actions the owner requests.

Processing, storage, and sharing

Google provides the Home MCP server and authorization flow. OpenAI provides ChatGPT and stores the connection credentials and conversations according to the owner's ChatGPT settings and OpenAI privacy policy. Google's handling of Home data is governed by the owner's Google settings and Google Privacy Policy. The owner does not operate a separate application server or database for this connection and does not sell Home data or share it with other users.

This website does not ask visitors to enter Home data and does not contain Home data, OAuth credentials, or access tokens.

Access and revocation

The ChatGPT connection is private to the owner's account. ChatGPT is set to ask before device-changing actions. The owner can disconnect the app in ChatGPT and revoke Google's Home access in the Google Home app or Google Account settings. The owner can manage or delete conversations using ChatGPT's controls. The owner does not keep a separate copy of Home data to retain or delete.
